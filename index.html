Why Lumen & Lens

The Ultimate Destination for Professional Photography Gear

Choosing the right camera starts with understanding how you shoot. Our catalogue brings together full-frame mirrorless bodies, responsive DSLRs, medium format systems and cinema rigs, each curated by working photographers. Compare sensor sizes, autofocus systems, burst rates and video specifications side by side, then narrow your shortlist with filters for mount, budget and resolution. Every product page includes field notes, sample images and compatibility guidance, so a new lens or body fits the kit you already own.

Still deciding? Our gear specialists answer questions by chat, phone or email, and our buying guides explain which sensor, lens mount and lighting modifier suits portraits, landscapes, weddings or commercial sets. Every purchase is covered by a manufacturer warranty, backed by our own two-year protection plan and a thirty-day return window. Repairs are handled by certified technicians who inspect, calibrate and document each camera before it returns to you.

Orders placed before 3pm ship the same business day from our warehouse. Delivery is insured, tracked and carbon-neutral, with free shipping over $199, plus support until your parcel arrives.
